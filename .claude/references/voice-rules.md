# Voice rules

Short rules for every skill in this repo. Skill-local "Hard rules" sections add to these; they do not replace them.

1. Em dashes (`—`) capped at about 1 per 100 words. Replace the excess with a comma, colon or parentheses, never a period. No en dashes between clauses, no double dashes.
2. Capitalize personal names, company names and product names.
3. Avoid AI vocabulary: `leverage`, `fundamentally`, `streamline`, `harness`, `delve`, `unlock`, `foster`, `robust`, `seamless`.
4. Specific numbers beat adjectives: `47%` beats `significant`. A number needs a named referent (what was measured, over what period).
5. First person, never third person. No "passionate", "driven" or "results-oriented".
6. Never invent a figure, date, client or result. If it is missing, ask, or drop the claim.
7. Never inflate an official title. Describe scope as work done, with numbers.
